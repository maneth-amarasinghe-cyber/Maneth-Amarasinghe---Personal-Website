FestConnect: Smart Festival Management System

FestConnect is a hypothetical Smart Festival Management System designed for a large-scale music festival in the UK. Developed for the Trends in Computer Science module offered by the University of Westminster through Informatics Institute of Technology (IIT Campus), the concept explores crowd control, ticketing, real-time alerts, and data analytics to improve event safety and user experience.

Working on this project provided an opportunity to explore system design and the role of technology in managing large crowds. A companion podcast was also created for people who prefer listening.

  • System design
  • Event management technology
  • Crowd safety concepts
  • Data analytics concepts
  • Podcast and communication
